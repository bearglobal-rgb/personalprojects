Privacy

What this website currently handles, what it does not collect, and the choices available to you.

Who this notice covers

This notice describes the current Swan Academy website, founded by Jota Swan. The legal operator’s name, business address and country will be published before live applications or payments open.

Our Contact page provides the currently available contact route. Please do not post private information in public social-media comments.

Information typed into previews

The email signup, application and contact forms are previews with submission disabled. This website does not send the information typed into those forms to a subscriber list, application database or support inbox. It does not store those answers in browser storage.

The checkout fields are visual examples. They do not collect card or billing information. The application-status examples are not linked to a person’s record.

Your browser may independently offer autofill or remember form entries under its own settings. Avoid entering sensitive information into a preview.

Information used to serve the website

Opening a page requires technical requests to the website’s host. The current preview is served through GitHub Pages. Hosting and network providers may process information such as your IP address, browser information, requested pages and request times to deliver and protect their services.

For information about GitHub’s practices, see the GitHub Privacy Statement. Hosting-provider records are not controlled by the browser preference settings on this website.

Browser storage and consent preferences

Swan Academy currently adds no optional analytics or advertising tools to this website. Fonts, logos and photographs are served with the site; viewing them does not require a request to Whop.

If you choose to save consent preferences, this website stores an essential-only preference and a save time in this browser’s local storage. It contains no name, email address or application answers. It stays on this browser until you reset it, clear browser storage, or the browser removes it.

You can save or remove that record on the Consent preferences page. Without a saved record, essential-only browsing remains the default.

External contact and social services

Opening an external link, such as Instagram, takes you to another service. If you send a message there, that service processes it under its own privacy rules. Do not send card details, identity documents or other sensitive information through social-media messages.

No live email-delivery provider, application database or payment checkout is connected to this preview. Their data practices will be described before those features open.

Before live services open

This notice will be updated to explain the information collected, why it is needed, applicable legal bases, service providers, retention arrangements and relevant international transfers before signup, application submission or payment is enabled.

Application communications and optional marketing subscriptions will be explained separately. Applying to Black will not automatically be treated as consent to unrelated marketing.

Your privacy requests

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, object to or restrict certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

Use Contact to ask about privacy or report a concern. A dedicated privacy inbox and request-handling process will be published before live collection starts. Response periods and any identity checks will follow the applicable requirements; no fixed response deadline is promised in this draft.

To remove a consent record from this browser, use Reset saved preference. That removes only the website’s saved preference, not records held by hosting or external services.

Changes to this notice

We will update this notice as the website changes. The revision date is shown at the top. Optional tracking will not be introduced under a claim that it was already active or covered by this preview’s essential-only setting.
