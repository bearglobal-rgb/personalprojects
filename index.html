  1. Step 01The Reset

    Deep Mimicry — Calibration

    Rebuild the ear. Pause every line, copy the subtitle, mimic out loud, isolate every word until the mouth owns it. Two hours minimum. Three is better.

  2. Step 02The ResetPermanent

    The Default Rule

    The entire digital life switches to English — devices, apps, search, AI, entertainment, news. This does not get reversed.

  3. Step 03The ResetPermanent

    Dead Time Immersion

    Whenever the ears are free, English is on. Not learning videos — the same content you would watch anyway. Boredom is banned.

  4. Step 04ActivationPermanent

    Shadow Boxing

    Talk out loud. Narrate. Argue with yourself. Simulate the room. The vocal cords have to move. Do not translate.

  5. Step 05Activation

    Deep Mimicry — Flow

    Same tools. Velocity instead of micro-managing. Easy lines, mimic and pass. Graduate when a full episode at normal speed needs only rare pauses.

  6. Step 06DepthPermanent

    Reading Out Loud

    A book you would read anyway. Every word spoken. Look up nested, in English. No flashcards. Fifteen to twenty pages a day.

  7. Step 07The Arena

    AI Sparring Partner

    Sixty minutes. Minute thirty is where easy vocabulary dies. Warm-up, deep-dive, debate. Three sessions a week. Daily if you can.

  8. Step 08The Arena

    Real People

    Unfiltered conversation. Do not judge fluency by the first thirty minutes — panic, then unlock, then rhythm. Discord. Two continuous hours.

  9. Step 09MasteryPermanent

    Writing

    Thirty minutes of honest sentences. Write first. Correct after. From this point, everything you type is English.

  10. Step 10Mastery

    The Content Creator

    Record a video. Do not start over. Watch it back. Hunt the errors. Re-record. One a week. Mastery is becoming your own teacher.

The origin

Who is Jota Swan?

I was not born inside the rooms that decide.

Rural Bahia. The work that travels, the capital that compounds, the conversations that open doors — they run in English.

I was outside that. That is the first fact.

School showed me the shadows. Not the language.

Years of grammar. Years of knowing the rule and still freezing when it counted.

That is the cave: you study the outline of English and call it fluency. I refused to stay there.

I stopped studying English. I started living inside it.

Devices. Media. Thought. Switched. Mimicry until the mouth owned the sound. Reading out loud. Sparring until easy vocabulary died.

No drills. If I did not enjoy it, I would not have stuck. That became the law.

English is not a subject. It is an operating language.

Highest-leverage skill I ever installed. Not to pass a test. To enter rooms that were closed.

Once it stopped being foreign, everything else moved — work, travel, the people you can stand next to.

I built the system I did not have.

Swan Academy is that sequence, written down. Ten protocols. Self-paced. Uncompromising.

I am a self-taught polyglot — English, and the Romance languages my students already speak. I do not run a school. I run a map.
